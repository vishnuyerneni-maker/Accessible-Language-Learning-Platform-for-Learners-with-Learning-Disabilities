## 🎓 Activity Guide

### 1. Phonics & Letter Sounds
**Best for**: Learning letter sounds

**How to use**:
1. Click "Phonics & Letter Sounds"
2. Select any letter (A-Z)
3. Click "🔊 Hear Letter Sound" to hear pronunciation
4. Or try "🎵 Word Blending Practice" to blend letters into words

**Features**:
- All 26 letters with sounds
- Example words for each letter
- 14 word blending exercises
- 3 speed options (slow/medium/fast)

---

### 2. Letter Tracing
**Best for**: Writing practice

**How to use**:
1. Click "Letter Tracing"
2. Choose a letter
3. Draw on the white canvas with your mouse or finger
4. Use "Show Template" to see the letter outline
5. Use "Clear" to start over

**Features**:
- Interactive canvas drawing
- Touch support for tablets
- Letter templates
- Audio feedback

---

### 3. Build Words
**Best for**: Spelling and word formation

**How to use**:
1. Click "Build Words"
2. Select a word to build
3. Drag the letter blocks to the empty boxes
4. Arrange them to spell the word
5. Click "Check Answer"

**Features**:
- 10 practice words
- Drag and drop interface
- Visual feedback (green = correct, red = wrong)
- Audio confirmation

---

### 4. Picture Matching
**Best for**: Visual learning and vocabulary

**How to use**:
1. Click "Picture Matching"
2. Click an emoji (like 🐱)
3. Then click its matching word (CAT)
4. Match all 6 pairs to win!

**Features**:
- 12 different picture-word pairs
- Score tracking
- Audio support
- Celebration when complete

---

### 5. Memory Match
**Best for**: Memory building

**How to use**:
1. Click "Memory Match"
2. Click cards to flip them over
3. Remember where each word is
4. Find both cards with the same word
5. Match all 6 pairs!

**Features**:
- 12 cards (6 matching pairs)
- 3D flip animation
- Score counter
- Memory challenge

---

### 6. Spelling Practice
**Best for**: Spelling with support

**How to use**:
1. Click "Spelling Practice"
2. Choose a category (Animals, Colors, Food, Family, or Actions)
3. Type the word you see
4. Click "Show Hint" if you need help
5. Click words in the Word Bank to auto-fill
6. Click "Check Spelling"

**Features**:
- 5 categories with multiple words each
- Spelling hints (first letter, pattern, length)
- Word bank for reference
- Audio pronunciation
- Instant feedback

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### Issue: "Learning content not opening"

**Solution 1**: Check Browser Console
1. Press `F12` to open Developer Tools
2. Click on "Console" tab
3. Look for any red error messages
4. Share these with support

**Solution 2**: Clear Cache and Reload
1. Press `Ctrl + F5` to hard refresh
2. Or go to Settings > Clear browsing data
3. Reload the page

**Solution 3**: Check File Paths
Make sure all these files exist in your folder:
- ✅ `script.js`
- ✅ `gamification.js`
- ✅ `phonics-module.js`
- ✅ `letter-tracing.js`
- ✅ `mini-games.js`
- ✅ `voice-features.js`
- ✅ `dyslexia-center.html`

**Solution 4**: Use Test Page
1. Open `test-dyslexia.html` in browser
2. Check which modules loaded successfully
3. Click test buttons to verify functionality

### Issue: "Audio not working"

**Solutions**:
- Check if your volume is turned up
- Allow browser permissions for audio
- Use Chrome or Edge for best support
- Make sure speakers/headphones are connected

### Issue: "Canvas not drawing"

**Solutions**:
- Wait 1-2 seconds after the modal opens
- Click directly on the white canvas area
- Try using a mouse instead of touchpad
- Click "Clear" and try again

### Issue: "Drag and drop not working"

**Solutions**:
- Click and hold on the letter block
- Drag directly to the empty box
- Make sure you're not dragging too fast
- Try refreshing the page
