# 🚀 Quick Start Guide - Dyslexia Learning Center

## How to Launch
1. **Double-click** `START_APP.bat`
2. Login with: `student` / `password123`
3. Click the **purple "Dyslexia Learning Center"** card at the top of your dashboard

---

## 6 Learning Activities Available

### 1. 🔤 Phonics & Letter Sounds
**Perfect for**: Beginning readers
- Learn all 26 letter sounds
- Practice word blending (cat, dog, sun, etc.)
- Three speed options: slow, medium, fast
- Audio feedback with visual highlighting

**Try this**: Click letter "B" to hear "bee, buh, Ball"

---

### 2. ✍️ Letter Tracing
**Perfect for**: Writing practice
- Draw letters on interactive canvas
- Faint letter templates to trace
- Beautiful purple gradient pen
- Works with mouse or touch

**Try this**: Trace letter "A" with your finger or mouse

---

### 3. 🧩 Word Builder
**Perfect for**: Spelling practice
- Drag and drop letters to build words
- 10 words available (CAT, DOG, SUN, MOON, etc.)
- Shuffled letters each time
- Audio confirmation when correct

**Try this**: Build the word "CAT" by dragging letters

---

### 4. 🎯 Picture Matching
**Perfect for**: Visual learning
- Match emojis with words
- 12 picture-word pairs
- Score tracking
- Audio support

**Try this**: Match 🐱 with "CAT"

---

### 5. 🧠 Memory Match
**Perfect for**: Memory building
- Find matching pairs of words
- 12 cards with 3D flip effect
- Remember card locations
- Celebrate when all pairs found

**Try this**: Flip cards to find "DOG" + "DOG"

---

### 6. ✏️ Spelling Practice
**Perfect for**: Word practice with support
- 5 categories: Animals, Colors, Food, Family, Actions
- Spelling hints available
- Word bank for reference
- Audio pronunciation

**Try this**: Spell "cat" with hints and word bank help

---

## Quick Tips

### ✨ Best Practices
- **Start with Phonics** if you're new to reading
- **Use hints** - there's no penalty!
- **Practice daily** to build streak and earn XP
- **Mix activities** to keep learning fun

### 🎯 Accessibility Tools
Bottom of every page:
- 🔊 Read Aloud
- 🎤 Voice Commands
- 👁️ Dyslexia Font
- 🔍 Larger Text

### 🏆 Earn Rewards
All activities give you XP points:
- Letter practice: +25 XP
- Word building: +50 XP
- Game completion: +100 XP

## Need Help?

### Audio Not Working?
1. Check browser permissions
2. Turn up device volume
3. Use Chrome or Edge browser

### Canvas Not Drawing?
1. Wait 1-2 seconds after opening
2. Click "Clear" button
3. Refresh page if needed
