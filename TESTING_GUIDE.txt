2. TESTING SCENARIOS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[TEST 1] ACCESSIBILITY FEATURES (Login Page)
   - Open index.html.
   - Click the "Theme" button in the top-right toolbar.
     > Expect: Colors change (Light -> Dark -> High Contrast). Toast message appears.
   - Click "Aa Font".
     > Expect: Font changes to a specific Dyslexia-friendly style (Comic Sans/Chalkboard).
   - Click "A+".
     > Expect: Text size increases.

[TEST 2] STUDENT JOURNEY (Core Flow)
   - Log in as 'student'.
   - Dashboard: Verify "English Greeting Basics" shows "Start Lesson" or progress.
   - Click "Start Lesson" or "Resume".
   - Lesson Player:
     - Click "Read Aloud".
       > Expect: Browser speaks the lesson text.
     - Click "Next: Take Quiz".
   - AI Quiz:
     > Expect: "✨ AI is generating..." text appears.
     > Expect: A unique question about 'English Greetings' loads.
     - Select the correct answer (usually hinted by options or obvious logic).
     > Expect: Feedback appears with a specific AI-generated EXPLANATION.
     - Click "Finish Quiz".
   - Return to Dashboard:
     > Expect: "English Greeting Basics" is 100% complete.
     > Expect: "Active Listening Skills" is now UNLOCKED (button active).

[TEST 3] PARENT MONITORING
   - Log out (if logged in).
   - Log in as 'parent'.
   - Parent Dashboard:
     - Check "Recent Activity Log".
     > Expect: You should see the recent actions from Test 2 (e.g., "Completed Quiz", "Updated progress to 100%").

[TEST 4] ERROR HANDLING
   - Login with "student" / "wrongpass".
     > Expect: Error message "Invalid Username or Password".
   - Disconnect Internet (simulate offline) and take Quiz.
     > Expect: Quiz falls back to the default "Offline Fallback" question.
